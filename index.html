My Interests

Space

Space

There is a lot of beauty on Earth which can be found in various places on Earth but If we look towards the stars there is so much more to discover.

Endless beauty with countless colours and complex shapes that define our universe with its natural flow.

The more we learn about space the more we learn how miniscule we are in the grand scheme of things.

I would say there is a lot of beauty in that.

But there is also hope that one day we will scale to the stars and fly among them.

Using the technology we create on Earth we will one day be able to explore every corner of our galaxy and find all its beauty and secrets.

Photography

Photography

Every now and then I stand infront of nature as if it was a painting and it was my duty to capture the moment the best I can.

Using the modern technology we have today I can capture the beauty of nature and save it so i can look at it and share it when I need boost.

There are so many things in the world to capture and I always want to have the right picture for any moment.

The things I like to photograph is mainly nature when it is at its most beautiful in its natural flow, Cats when they are the cutest and Cities when the lighting is just right.

My current collection is broad but there are still situations where I wish I had the right picture for the moment.

For example, Vertical photos are rare in my gallery so I wish I could capture some more.

Travel

Travel

To really experience life you need to experience all of it with a natural flow and from every corner of the globe.

Without going to different countries you cant experience the different cultures and climates of the world.

To live life and never experience the thrill of Skiing or the smell of the ocean wouldnt be a life fully lived.

The different cuisine of the world and the creativity of cultures when creating a new dish has to be experienced.

The architecture from different countries from cobblestone paths to paved highways going on for miles.

Different norms to shatter what is seen as normal and give more perspective to the world.
